# Problem Statement 26007

## Title

**Safe and Efficient Operation of Mine Vehicles in Fog and Low-Visibility Conditions in Open Cast Iron Ore Mines**

## Background

NMDC Limited is India’s largest iron ore producer, with an annual production capacity of approximately 53 Million Tonnes Per Annum (MTPA) from its three fully mechanized mining complexes:

- BIOM–Kirandul Complex, Chhattisgarh
- BIOM–Bacheli Complex, Chhattisgarh
- Donimalai Complex, Karnataka

The Bailadila Region contributes nearly 37 MTPA of iron ore production. In alignment with the National Steel Policy, NMDC has set a target of achieving a production capacity of 100 MT by 2030, with approximately 80 MT expected from the Bailadila Sector.

The Bailadila mining region experiences severe monsoon conditions from June to October. Heavy rainfall, strong winds, high humidity, dense clouds, and thick fog are common during this period. Visibility on mine haul roads, particularly in hilltop mining areas, can reduce to as little as **3–5 meters**.

These conditions create significant challenges for the safe and efficient operation of Heavy Earth Moving Machinery (HEMM), particularly dumpers used for ore transportation.

## Problem Description

Dense fog and extremely low visibility during the monsoon season create serious safety and operational challenges in Bailadila iron ore mines.

When visibility is severely restricted, dumper operators are required to reduce vehicle speed or temporarily stop operations to maintain safety. This directly affects mining productivity by increasing haul cycle times, reducing fleet utilization, slowing ore evacuation, and potentially causing production losses.

At the same time, low-visibility conditions significantly increase the risk of:

- Vehicle-to-vehicle collisions
- Accidents involving mine machinery
- Road-side and haul-road incidents
- Delays in material transportation
- Operational disruptions
- Reduced fleet productivity

Conventional visibility aids and existing operational controls may have limited effectiveness when fog becomes extremely dense, particularly in areas where visibility falls to only a few meters.

The challenge is therefore to enable the **safe, reliable, and efficient movement of mine vehicles under severe fog and low-visibility conditions**, while minimizing operational disruptions and maintaining continuity of mining activities.

## Expected Solution Requirements

The proposed solution is expected to improve:

- Operator situational awareness
- Vehicle guidance
- Collision-risk awareness and avoidance
- Real-time monitoring of mine vehicles
- Decision-making for safe haul-road operations
- Operational continuity during adverse weather conditions

The solution may leverage technologies such as:

- AI/ML-based analytics
- Computer vision and thermal imaging
- LiDAR and radar-based sensing
- GPS/DGPS-based vehicle tracking
- Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) communication
- Vehicle-to-Infrastructure (V2I) communication
- Autonomous or driver-assistance systems
- IoT-enabled monitoring systems
- Centralized command and control platforms
- Digital Twin-based operational monitoring

## Expected Outcomes

The solution is expected to contribute to:

1. Improved safety of dumper operations during foggy and low-visibility conditions.
2. Reduced risk of collisions and operational accidents.
3. Improved haulage efficiency and reduced haul cycle times.
4. Enhanced fleet utilization during adverse weather conditions.
5. Greater continuity of mining operations during the monsoon season.
6. Reduced production losses caused by extremely low visibility.
7. Real-time monitoring and improved decision support for vehicle operators and control-room personnel.
8. A scalable and deployable approach suitable for large-scale mechanized open-cast mining operations.
